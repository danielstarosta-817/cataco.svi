🌀 Seismic hazard proxy ▼
This is a distance-decay MMI-style estimate centered on the Veroes epicenter (~10.43°N, 68.94°W) — not an official ShakeMap product. It is calibrated so La Guaira, Distrito Capital, and Carabobo score high despite distance, reflecting documented prolonged shaking (>60 seconds) attributed to soil amplification in the Caracas/La Guaira valley-and-coast system.
