# Badstars photo sourcing brief

Audit of `index.html`: 32 curated case studies total, 16 already fully illustrated (all 33 existing image files are already wired in — nothing orphaned). The 16 below have no photo yet. For each: the specific real-world subject worth photographing, the best leads found, and the filename/placement to use once you have the file.

**A technical note first:** I could not download the actual image bytes into this folder. My sandbox's network is allowlisted and blocks image hosts (Wikimedia/Commons, stock-photo CDNs, etc.) even though web search and article text fetches work fine — so I could confirm *which* photos exist and where, but not pull the files themselves. You (or a Claude in Chrome browsing session, which runs on your real network) can grab these directly and drop them in this folder; I can then wire them into the HTML immediately, same as the other 33.

### 1. Tohoku, Japan (`tohoku`)
Three separate images needed for the three sub-stories:
- **Street mural in a still-demolished Tohoku town** — search "Tohoku tsunami recovery mural" or check the Rikuzentakata/Kesennuma mural projects.
- **Okawa Elementary School ruins**, Ishinomaki — preserved as a memorial since 2021. Wikipedia: [Okawa Elementary School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okawa_Elementary_School). Also Getty Images has many.
- **Lapras, Miyagi's tsunami-recovery mascot Pokémon** — great source image already located: `https://soranews24.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/1_3a195c.jpg` from [SoraNews24's article](https://soranews24.com/2019/07/10/pokemon-lapras-appears-on-streets-in-lakes-of-miyagi-as-part-of-new-tourism-ambassador-role/).
- Filenames: `tohoku-mural.jpg`, `okawa-elementary-ruins.jpg`, `lapras-miyagi-mascot.jpg`

### 2. Banda Aceh, Indonesia (`aceh`)
- **Tsunami early-warning tower/siren**, Banda Aceh. Getty/iStock have stock photos ("Tsunami Warning Siren," "Tsunami Aceh"); Lonely Planet's [Siron Tsunami Memorial Park](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/indonesia/sumatra/banda-aceh/attractions/siron-tsunami-memorial-park/a/poi-sig/1587300/356619) page is a good freely-licensed-photo lead too.
- Filename: `aceh-warning-tower.jpg`

### 3. Myeik Archipelago, Myanmar (`moken`)
- **Moken kabang boats / stilt village**. Survival International's gallery ["The ocean is our universe"](https://www.survivalinternational.org/galleries/moken-sea-gypsies) is the strongest single source.
- Filename: `moken-kabang-boats.jpg`

### 4. Marshall Islands (`marshall-islands`)
- **A stick chart** (wave-navigation chart made of coconut-frond ribs and shells). Wikimedia Commons Category:Stick charts has several; the Smithsonian and Bishop Museum both hold originals that are well photographed. Try [World History Commons](https://worldhistorycommons.org/marshall-islands-stick-chart) or [Smarthistory](https://smarthistory.org/chart-marshall/) as freely-fetchable pages with photos.
- Filename: `marshall-stick-chart.jpg`

### 5. Solomon Islands (`solomon`)
- **2007 tsunami damage** — a specific usable file already identified: Wikimedia Commons [File:Tsunami damage, Solomon Islands 2007. Photo- AusAID](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tsunami_damage,_Solomon_Islands_2007._Photo-_AusAID_(10730788596).jpg) — AusAID/DFAT credited, clean provenance.
- Filename: `solomon-tsunami-2007.jpg`

### 6. Thailand (`thailand`)
- **Maikhao Beach, Phuket** (where Tilly Smith gave her warning) — no specific historical photo of the event surfaced, but the beach itself and news photos of Tilly Smith are widely available; try Wikipedia's [Tilly Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilly_Smith) article page.
- Filename: `maikhao-beach-thailand.jpg`

### 7. Nara, Japan and Nias, Indonesia (`vernacular-architecture`)
Two images:
- **Hōryū-ji five-story pagoda**, Nara — its central pillar (shinbashira) is the earthquake-engineering story. Many freely licensed shots exist; [Horyuji's own site](https://www.horyuji.or.jp/en/garan/gojyunoto/) or Wikipedia's [Hōryū-ji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C5%8Dry%C5%AB-ji) article.
- **Omo hada / omo sebua traditional house**, Bawömataluo village, Nias — Wikipedia's [Omo sebua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omo_sebua) and [Bawomataluo village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bawomataluo_village) pages have photos.
- Filenames: `horyuji-pagoda.jpg`, `nias-omo-hada-house.jpg`

### 8. Santa Cruz, California (`santa-cruz`)
- **Pacific Garden Mall collapse, 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake** — Santa Cruz Public Libraries' local history archive has a well-documented, citable set: [Closed-off Pacific Garden Mall after the earthquake](https://history.santacruzpl.org/omeka/items/show/10340) (Calisphere mirror also available).
- Filename: `pacific-garden-mall-1989.jpg`

### 9. Oakland, California (`oakland-berkeley`)
- **1991 Oakland Hills firestorm** — Wikimedia Commons [File:Oakland firestorm 1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oakland_firestorm_1.jpg) (NASA DART sourced) is a clean, direct hit.
- Filename: `oakland-firestorm-1991.jpg`

### 10. Puerto Rico (`puerto-rico`)
- **Festival de Santiago Apóstol, Loíza** — bomba dancers and coconut-shell vejigante masks. Leads: [Raíces Cultural Center Digital Archive](https://archive.raicesculturalcenter.org/collections/show/1), [Google Arts & Culture vejigante mask entry](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/vejigante-mask-festivities-of-santiago-apostle-carved-and-polychrome-coconut-lo%C3%ADza-puerto-rico/WwG4lTThoNbJ8w?hl=en).
- Filename: `loiza-vejigante-festival.jpg`

### 11. Mali, Niger, and the Sahel (`griot-sahel`)
- **A griot performing** (kora or balafon). Wikimedia Commons [Category:Griots](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Griots) has many freely licensed portraits.
- Filename: `griot-musician.jpg`

### 12. Haiti (`haiti`)
- **A Port-au-Prince "gingerbread house"** — the hurricane-adapted vernacular style that wasn't built for the 2010 earthquake. TIME's [Haiti's Gingerbread Houses](https://time.com/archive/7061471/haitis-gingerbread-houses/) photo essay and [World Monuments Fund](https://www.wmf.org/monuments/gingerbread-neighborhood) both have strong images.
- Filename: `haiti-gingerbread-house.jpg`

### 13. Iquique and Constitución, Chile (`chile-half-house`)
- **Quinta Monroy, Iquique** — Alejandro Aravena/Elemental's "half a good house," photographed by Cristóbal Palma. Good entry points: [MoMA's Quinta Monroy page](https://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2010/smallscalebigchange/projects/quinta_monroy_housing.html), [Dezeen's 2025 retrospective](https://www.dezeen.com/2025/01/10/quinta-monroy-housing-elemental-alejandro-aravena-21st-century-architecture/).
- Filename: `quinta-monroy-iquique.jpg`

### 14. Corinth, Greece (`poseidon-greece`)
- **Temple of Poseidon at Isthmia** (the archaeological site itself, not the more famous Sounion temple). Wikimedia Commons [Category:Archaeological site of Isthmia, Poseidon Temple](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Archaeological_site_of_Isthmia,_Poseidon_Temple).
- Filename: `isthmia-poseidon-temple.jpg`

### 15. Kobe and Tokyo, Japan (`japan-quake-day`)
- **Kobe Luminarie** light display — clean subject, tons of freely available photos, e.g. via [Kobe Luminarie Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Luminarie) or travel-guide sites (GaijinPot, Japan Travel official site).
- Optional second image: a September 1st Disaster Prevention Day drill photo, if you want both registers illustrated.
- Filename: `kobe-luminarie.jpg`

### 16. Cairo, Egypt (`nile-egypt`)
- **The Nilometer on Rhoda Island** — best identified file: Wikimedia Commons [File:Cairo Nilometer 2.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cairo_Nilometer_2.jpg) or [File:Kairo Nilometer BW 1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kairo_Nilometer_BW_1.jpg).
- Filename: `nilometer-rhoda-island.jpg`

### 17. Lisbon, Portugal (`lisbon`)
- **Baixa Pombalina's rebuilt grid**, ideally an aerial or hillside view showing the geometric street plan against Lisbon's older, organic surroundings. iStock has many; for a freely reusable option try Wikipedia's [Pombaline Lower Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pombaline_Lower_Town) article.
- Filename: `baixa-pombalina-lisbon.jpg`

## How to finish this once you have files

Drop the images into `/badstars/` using the filenames above (or any name — just tell me what you used). Each case's story block in `index.html` is a `story:` template literal containing one or more `<div class="story-section">` blocks; I'll add a `<figure class="story-img"><img src="..." alt="..."><figcaption>...</figcaption></figure>` in the right spot for each one, matching the style of the 33 images already in place.
